Natural language processing is a field of artificial intelligence that helps computers understand human language. It combines computational linguistics with statistical and machine learning models. Early systems relied on hand-written rules, but modern systems learn patterns from large collections of text. Common applications include machine translation, sentiment analysis, chatbots and text summarization. One of the main challenges is ambiguity, because the same word or sentence can have different meanings in different contexts. Researchers address this problem by training models on context, so that the meaning of a word depends on the words around it.
